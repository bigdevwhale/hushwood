## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-21

- Sit quietly in a pixel-art place: animals come out as the quiet builds and run when the phone moves.
- Four places (forest clearing, lake, mountain meadow, shore) with 38 animals, their own hours and seasons, and rare guests.
- A guardian animal read from how you practise, its trust, gifts, and a portrait painted one sitting at a time.
- Breathing rhythms, the places' sounds from real field recordings (on and off right in the sitting) and a bell; dimming when face down.
- Offerings, a field journal with a herbarium and the year's rings, a widget and a daily reminder.
- English and Russian.
